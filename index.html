Our Policies

Privacy, Return, Exchange & Refund Policy

Last Updated: 21 November 2026
Welcome to SHOPYHAT!

We value your trust and are committed to providing a reliable and convenient shopping experience. Please read our policies carefully before placing an order.

01

Privacy Policy

SHOPYHAT respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

1.1 Information We Collect

When you contact us or place an order, we may collect:

  • Customer name
  • Phone number
  • Delivery address
  • Order and product details
  • Information voluntarily shared during customer communication

1.2 How We Use Your Information

  • To process and confirm orders.
  • To arrange product delivery.
  • To communicate about orders and customer support.
  • To handle returns, exchanges and complaints.
  • To improve our products and shopping experience.

1.3 Information Sharing

We do not sell customers' personal information. Necessary information may be shared with delivery partners and service providers to fulfil orders.

1.4 Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect customer information against unauthorized access, misuse or disclosure.

1.5 Customer Privacy Rights

Customers may contact SHOPYHAT to request correction or deletion of their personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements and necessary transaction records.

02

Return Policy

Customer satisfaction is important to us. We aim to ensure that every customer receives the product they ordered in proper condition.

3-Day Return Request Period

Customers must contact SHOPYHAT within 3 calendar days of receiving the product to request a return.

2.1 Eligible Reasons for Return

  • The delivered product is damaged or defective.
  • The wrong product, colour or design is delivered.
  • The product is significantly different from the confirmed order description.

2.2 Change of Mind

We do not accept returns simply because a customer changes their mind, dislikes the selected style or colour, or no longer wants an accurately delivered product.

This restriction does not affect any rights available to customers under applicable consumer protection laws.

2.3 Product Condition

  • The product should remain unused and unwashed.
  • Original tags, labels and packaging should be retained where applicable.
  • Clear photos or videos may be requested to verify the issue.
  • The product may be inspected before approval.
03

Exchange Policy

Exchange requests are accepted for eligible damaged, defective or incorrectly delivered products.

3.1 Exchange Eligibility

  • Wrong product received.
  • Damaged or defective product received.
  • Product significantly different from the confirmed order.

3.2 Saree & Clothing Exchange

For sarees, including Tangail sarees, and other clothing items:

  • Please review product descriptions and details before confirming an order.
  • Minor colour variations may occur due to photography, lighting and screen settings.
  • Genuine product defects or incorrect deliveries will be reviewed.

3.3 Non-Eligible Exchanges

  • Products that have been used, washed or altered.
  • Products damaged through customer misuse or improper handling.
  • Requests based only on a change of mind.
04

Return & Exchange Process

To request a return or exchange:

  1. Contact SHOPYHAT within 3 calendar days of receiving your order.
  2. Provide your order number and registered contact details.
  3. Explain the problem clearly.
  4. Share photos or videos of the damaged, defective or incorrect product.
  5. Wait for our team to review your request and provide instructions.
Important:

Please do not send products back before receiving confirmation and return instructions from SHOPYHAT.

05

Delivery Charges

If a return or exchange is approved because SHOPYHAT delivered a damaged, defective or incorrect product, SHOPYHAT will bear the applicable return or exchange delivery charges.

Any other delivery-charge arrangements will be communicated to the customer before proceeding.

06

Refund Policy

If a refund is approved:

  • The customer will be informed about the refund process.
  • The refund will be processed through an agreed payment method.
  • The applicable refund amount and estimated processing time will be communicated to the customer.

Refunds will be handled in accordance with applicable laws and the agreed payment arrangements.

07

Policy Updates

SHOPYHAT may update these policies when necessary. Updated versions will be published through our official online channels.

Customers are encouraged to review these policies before placing an order.

Need Help?

For privacy-related questions, order assistance, return or exchange requests, contact our team.
